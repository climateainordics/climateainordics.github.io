**Two years ago, on 24 October 2024, we started Climate AI Nordics.** What began as an idea to connect Nordic researchers working on AI for climate and the environment has grown into a community of [272 researchers in the Nordics from 119 organisations](/people/), plus 146 supporting affiliates from outside the Nordics. Thank you all for being part of it!

To celebrate, we have given our website a makeover. Below we first look back at some highlights from our second year, and then show what is new on the website.

## Highlights from our second year

- **AICC at EurIPS 2025.** In December we co-organized the workshop _AI for Climate and Conservation_ at the first EurIPS conference in Copenhagen. [Read the reflections](/news/2025-12-15-cain-at-eurips/).
- **Our own YouTube channel.** We launched an [official YouTube channel](/news/2025-11-25-youtube-channel/), so that you can watch recordings of our webinars whenever it suits you.
- **A formal partnership with Climate Change AI.** In May we [partnered with Climate Change AI](/news/2026-05-08-ccai-partnership/), and we were also a [network partner of the CCAI 2026 virtual summer school](/news/2026-07-07-ccai-summer-school-with-cain/).
- **The 2nd Nordic Workshop on AI for Climate.** In June, researchers, practitioners and industry representatives gathered in Copenhagen for a full day of keynotes, talks, posters and discussions, including lunch under the trees in a nearby park. [Read the recap](/news/2026-06-26-nordic-workshop-recap/).
- **An official non-profit.** In August, Climate AI Nordics was [registered as a non-profit association](/news/2026-08-04-cain-ngo/) (_ideell förening_) in Sweden, which gives the network a solid foundation for the years ahead.
- **AICC-2 at ECCV 2026.** In September we co-organized the second edition of AICC, this time at ECCV in Malmö. [Read the recap](/news/2026-09-16-aicc2eccv-recap/).
- **Looking ahead.** The [3rd Nordic Workshop on AI for Climate](/news/2026-09-15-cain-2027-event-teaser/) will take place in Oslo on 22 April 2027. Save the date!

On top of this, we have kept up our monthly newsletters, webinars, featured works and featured members. A big thank you to all speakers, authors, organizers and partners who made this possible.

## An improved website for our next chapter

With a growing network and more and more content, our old website had started to feel cramped: long pages of text, small images and few ways to find what you were looking for. So, as a birthday present to ourselves (and hopefully to you), we have redesigned it in several ways.

![The Climate AI Nordics homepage before and after the redesign](/images/posts/2026-10-26-two-years/homepage-before-after.jpg)

### A visual style built from our logo

The new look starts from our globe logo. The **teal and sage-green colours** are taken directly from it, so the website, the logo and our other material now belong together. We use two clear, modern typefaces: _Manrope_ for headings and _Inter_ for body text. Content is organized in **cards** with images, which makes it much quicker to scan. And instead of a stock landscape, the homepage now shows **real photos from our own events**, because the people are what makes this network.

### A homepage that tells you what is going on

The homepage now leads with who we are and three clear ways in: join the network, see upcoming events, or meet the people. Right below are **live numbers about the network**, which update automatically as new members join. After that come cards for upcoming events, the latest news, featured works and job openings.

### Filter the news

The [News](/news/) page brings together news, featured works, featured members and job openings. You can now **filter by type with one click**, for example to see only job openings.

![The News page with filter buttons for news, featured works, featured members and job openings](/images/posts/2026-10-26-two-years/news-filters.jpg)

### Events that are easier to follow

The [Events](/events/) page is now split into **Upcoming** and **Past events**, and past events with a recording get a "Recording" label. Each event page has a clear box with the **date, weekday, time and location**. All our recorded talks are collected on the [recordings page](/recorded-events/).

![An event page before and after: the new design shows the date, weekday and time in a clear box](/images/posts/2026-10-26-two-years/event-before-after.jpg)

### Designed for your phone, too

Many of you read our news on your phone, so every page has been checked on small screens. The menu folds into a compact button, the pages no longer scroll sideways, and the old sidebar no longer pushes the content down.

![The homepage on a phone before and after the redesign](/images/posts/2026-10-26-two-years/mobile-before-after.jpg)

### And much more

- **Sharper images** throughout the news and event lists.
- A **"More news / More events"** section at the end of each post, with related posts.
- A new **footer** with quick links, contact details and our social media channels.
- Cleaner [About](/about/), [Join](/join/), [Follow](/follow/) and [Partners](/partners/) pages, with clear buttons and a card for each of our partners.

We hope you like the updated website! If something doesn't work as expected, or if you have ideas for improvements, please let us know at [contact@climateainordics.com](mailto:contact@climateainordics.com).

Here's to the next year of AI for climate action across the Nordics, and see you in Oslo!
