title: Recap of the AICC-2 workshop at ECCV 2026 in Malmö
---

<br />

The [**2nd Workshop on AI for Climate an Conservation (AICC-2)**](https://sites.google.com/g.harvard.edu/aicc2eccv26/), organized by Climate AI Nordics, took place on Sept 9th at [ECCV 2026](https://eccv.ecva.net/) in Malmö (Sweden), bringing together a packed room of
researchers and practitioners working at the intersection of AI, climate, and conservation. 

During this full-day event, the attendants got to listen to a great lineup of keynotes, where [Devis Tuia](https://people.epfl.ch/devis.tuia), [Nora Gourmelon](https://www.gourmelon.de/), [Bruno Sanchez-Andrade Nuño](https://brunosan.eu/),
[Abdulhakim M. Abdi](https://hakimabdi.com/) provided insightful and engaging presentations and discussions on topics such as video-based biodiversity monitoring (Devis), computer vision for monitoring the changing Arctic (Nora),
[geoAI-vs-just-AI](https://brunosan.eu/posts/what-if-there-is-no-geoai-just-ai/) (Bruno), and what is really needed for AI to deliver for nature conservation (Abdulhakim). These topics were further expanded on in an engaging panel discussion.
The day also included a diverse set of oral & poster presentations.

On the evening after AICC-2, Climate AI Nordics also organized a vibrant [AI for planetary health social](https://climateainordics.com/events/2026-08-13-eccv26-social/) at Cantin in Malmö. This was done in collaboration with [Asterisk Labs](https://asterisk.coop/),
as well as the [CV for marine vision](https://vap.aau.dk/marinevision/) and [CV4Ecology](https://cv4e-workshop.github.io/) communities (cudos to the people behind the organization: Mikolaj Czerkawski, Aleksis Pirinen, Rupa Kurinchi-Vendhan, Malte Pedersen,
Jacqueline Campbell, Olof Mogren, Ankit Kariryaa, Nico Lang)! It was really great to see so many people working on these topics get together!
