We are happy to announce the full program for the [**2nd Workshop on AI for Climate and Conservation (AICC-2)**](https://sites.google.com/g.harvard.edu/aicc2eccv26/) at [ECCV 2026](https://eccv.ecva.net/)!

The workshop, a **full-day event on Sept 9th**, will bring together researchers working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, climate science, ecology, conservation, Earth observation, and environmental monitoring. The program features a diverse set of keynote talks,
contributed research presentations, a panel discussion, and a poster session designed to foster scientific exchange and collaboration.

Highlights of the program include keynote presentations covering topics such as:

- UAV-based environmental monitoring and AI for ecological applications
- Reliable AI for understanding environmental change in the Arctic
- Earth observation, geospatial foundation models, and large-scale environmental monitoring
- The role of AI in advancing nature conservation beyond model performance alone

The workshop will also feature contributed oral presentations on a wide range of emerging topics, including:

- Climate physics and weather-related machine learning
- Precipitation forecasting with neural differential equation models
- Interpretable AI for wildfire forecasting
- Remote sensing change detection and reasoning
- Flood-impact prediction in urban environments
- Self-supervised and predictive representation learning for Earth observation data

In addition to the scientific presentations, participants will have the opportunity to engage in a panel discussion with invited speakers, exchange ideas during networking breaks, and present their latest work during the poster session.

We look forward to welcoming researchers, practitioners, and students interested in leveraging AI to address some of the most pressing challenges in climate and conservation.
