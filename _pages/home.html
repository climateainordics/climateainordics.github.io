Who we are

We are already in a climate emergency, causing biodiversity loss, extreme weather and human suffering. Climate AI Nordics connects researchers across the Nordic countries to share expertise and resources, and to develop AI-based tools that inform the decisions needed for a sustainable future: from rewilding and rewetting to reducing emissions and the reliance on fossil fuels.
