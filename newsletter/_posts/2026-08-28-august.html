Recent events


More is different: emergent social conventions and tipping points in AI populations

This event took place 2026-08-27. Webinar with Ariel Flint Ashery, City St George’s, University of London. Social conventions are the foundation of social coordination, shaping how individuals come together to form a society. In this talk, I will present theoretical and experimental findings that demonstrate the spontaneous emergence of social norms in LLM populations, as well as the existence of tipping points in social convention. I will show that agentic AI populations can establish social conventions and highlight how collective biases can emerge even when individual agents appear unbiased. I will conclude by stressing how the ability of AI agents to develop norms without explicit programming has significant implications for designing AI systems that align with human values and societal goals.

Climate Change AI Community: Meet Idriss, Bridging Blue Carbon, Community Conservation, and AI

2026-08-26 Idriss Duramany Koromah is the Executive Director of Coastal Communities for Mangrove Protection – Sierra Leone (CCMP-SL), leading mangrove restoration, coastal resilience, and women’s livelihood initiatives across more than 50 coastal communities. Holding an MSc in Rural Development and Natural Resource Management from SLU in Sweden, he brings expertise in nature-based solutions, FPIC-based community engagement, and Verra VCS+CCB certified blue carbon finance. Idriss is now joining the Climate Change AI community to explore how AI can advance satellite-based mangrove monitoring, enhance blue carbon MRV, and make community-generated field data accessible to funders while preserving local ownership.
