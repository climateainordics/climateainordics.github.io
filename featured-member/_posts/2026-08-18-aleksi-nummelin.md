**Hi Aleksi! Could you tell us a bit about yourself and your work?**

I have a background in ocean and climate dynamics and I have worked mostly with Earth System Models and satellite data throughout my career. Since moving to my current position at the Finnish Meteorological Institute, I have also become more linked to coastal modelling, with topics ranging from marine heat waves to impacts of renewable energy development on the environment.

**What kinds of research opportunities or collaborations are you excited to be part of in the future?**

Since the coastal ocean is very challenging to simulate, I have become more and more excited about learning directly from observation, and not just simulations as is often the case. At the moment I am working to create models for high horizontal resolution (~100 m) coastal sea surface temperature reconstructions in complex terrain using satellite images, and in the future, I would be interested in expanding the approach to other variables, such as sea surface height.

**Is there anything else you would like to share with the Climate AI Nordics network?**

I am very interested in applying ocean ML models in different science and operational applications. Unfortunately, we have limited resources to develop ML models ourselves, so if you have a model that you have developed and think could be interesting to test in the Baltic Sea, get in touch!

**What is the best way for people to get in touch with you?**

Send me an [email](aleksi.nummelin@fmi.fi) and hope for the best! Most of the time, I will also answer on my phone, so don't hesitate to call me if I've managed to ignore your email. If the topic is code related, I will also reply in [GitHub](github.com/AleksiNummelin).
