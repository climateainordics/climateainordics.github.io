The postdoctoral researcher will develop a data-driven screening workflow for biomaterial design, combining molecular representations, machine learning, physics-based simulations, and experimental data. The role involves working with an interdisciplinary team to predict how chemical modifications influence the properties of wood-based biopolymers and establishing classical baselines for quantum computing comparisons.

The position is based in Espoo, Finland, and involves collaboration with an interdisciplinary team of experts from chemistry, mathematics, and physics, as well as a dedicated group of quantum physicists.

Key qualifications:
- A doctoral degree in a relevant field such as computer science, data science, machine learning, chemical or material informatics, or a closely related discipline, completed no more than five years ago.
- Strong practical experience in data-driven modelling and at least one of the following: molecular representations, quantitative structure-activity relationships, or chemical or material informatics.
- Ability to work with molecular representations and connect molecular structure to measured or simulated material properties.
- Strong programming skills in Python or a comparable scientific programming language, with experience in developing documented and reproducible computational workflows.
- A strong scientific track record relevant to the position and the ability to produce high-quality scientific outputs.
- Fluency in English and the ability to work in an international, multidisciplinary research environment.
