The postdoc will work on developing methods and supporting software for analysis, modelling, optimisation and control of electric power systems from a system perspective. The project involves building a national digital twin of the Swedish power system with open datasets and demonstrators for data exchange between grid companies and flexible customers.

The position is based in Stockholm, Sweden. The work is carried out in close collaboration with industry and grid operators.

Key qualifications:
- A doctoral degree or an equivalent foreign degree in electric power engineering or a closely related field, with a focus on modelling, machine learning or optimisation at the power system level.
- Documented experience of software development for the analysis of large datasets, for example in Python or Julia.
- Good command of Swedish and English, spoken and written.
- Preferred qualifications include a doctoral degree obtained within the last three years prior to the application deadline, documented research expertise in the field, evidenced by publications, and good knowledge of open source solutions in the field.
