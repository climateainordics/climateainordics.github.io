{%- comment -%} Prints an event time as written in front matter. Unquoted YAML times like `event_time: 09:00` are parsed as minutes (540), so convert those back to HH:MM. Usage: {% include event-time.html time=page.event_time %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign t = include.time | append: "" -%} {%- if t != "" -%} {%- unless t contains ":" -%} {%- assign hh = include.time | divided_by: 60 | floor | prepend: "0" | slice: -2, 2 -%} {%- assign mm = include.time | modulo: 60 | floor | prepend: "0" | slice: -2, 2 -%} {%- assign t = hh | append: ":" | append: mm -%} {%- endunless -%} {%- endif -%} {{- t -}}
