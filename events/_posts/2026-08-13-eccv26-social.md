**Attending ECCV 2026 in Malmö and interested in using AI to better understand, protect, and restore our planet?**

Join us for an informal evening social bringing together researchers, practitioners, students, and innovators working at the intersection of computer vision, AI, climate,
biodiversity, conservation, and oceans. Whether you're presenting a paper during ECCV, looking for collaborators, exploring new research directions, or
simply interested in meeting others working on AI for planetary health, we'd love to see you there.

📍 Location: Cantin, Malmö

🕖 Time: 19:00–22:00

💳 Format: Casual afterwork / happy hour (self-paid food and drinks)

This community event is jointly organized by:
* [Climate AI Nordics](https://climateainordics.com/)
* [ECCV 2026 Marine Computer Vision Workshop](https://vap.aau.dk/marinevision/)
* [AI for Conservation / CV4Ecology](https://cv4e-workshop.github.io/)
* [Asterisk Labs](https://asterisk.coop/)

The goal is simple: create a welcoming space for the growing community applying computer vision and AI to environmental challenges across land, ocean, climate, and biodiversity.

We look forward to meeting both familiar faces and new members of the community during ECCV 2026!
